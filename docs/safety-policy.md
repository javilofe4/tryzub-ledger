# Safety Policy

Tryzub Ledger is built for open-source intelligence documentation and analysis.

## Purpose

The platform is intended to document public events, claims, incidents, losses, source contradictions, and geospatial information with attribution.

## No targeting guidance

Tryzub Ledger does not provide tactical recommendations, targeting assistance, or instructions for harming people.
The platform is not a battlefield planning tool.

The application must not produce live operational guidance, suggest targets, optimize attacks, or transform OSINT into instructions for harm.

## Sensitive media

Sensitive media may be flagged in the data model, and human review is required before dissemination.

## Responsible use

Users should apply OSINT ethics and legal norms when collecting, storing, and sharing information.

## Independence

Tryzub Ledger is independent and is not affiliated with the Ukrainian government, UNITED24, NATO, any military, or any intelligence service. Official Ukraine references and donations should go through https://u24.gov.ua/.
